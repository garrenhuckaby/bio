## The idea
Combine fingerprint access, scheduled unlocking, and dose logging with a web dashboard.

## System
The prototype brings together an ESP32, fingerprint sensor, load cell, and an actuator, with a Supabase-backed application.

## Project notes
A full case study will cover the hardware architecture, design choices, and lessons from developing the prototype. No patient or pilot data is included here.
