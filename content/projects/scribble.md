## The idea
Capture handwriting using infrared light and a compact tracking system, with the emitter close to the pen tip.

## Engineering questions
- How does camera geometry affect tracking precision across a writing surface?
- How can a small battery support the sensing and infrared electronics?
- What mechanical design keeps the pen comfortable to use?

## Current work
Exploring optical geometry, power electronics, and a custom PCB. This is an experimental project; tracking accuracy has not yet been validated.
